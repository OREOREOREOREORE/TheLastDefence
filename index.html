About & How to play

The Last Defence is a HongKong-style mini cooperative game where you and your partner work together to defend your base.

After you hit the Start button, you will be put into an existing game room with another player, or a new room if there is no available room.

Hit the Ready button when you're ready. The game will start when both of you are ready.

Mission: Collaborate with your partner to defend your BASE in the middle for 3 MINS!

The mission is successful if both the BASE and any ONE player survive after 3 mins.

You lose otherwise. That is, both of you die OR the BASE is destroyed before the time ends.

Monsters will spawn from the edges of the map and move towards you and your BASE

Be careful! Monsters will move to you instead if they see you. They will attack when they reach you or the BASE .

Don't worry! You could kill the monsters by shooting 3 egg puffs to them!

Difficulty is increasing! The monsters will spam more and more. In the last 30s of the game, the monsters will activate Dark Mode.

Luckily, you can collect EXP and level up for every monster you kill. By level up, you gain some mystery ability, which may increase your ability to kill the monsters, move faster or even a larger HP!

Game Control

Moving: Press 'WASD' or arrow keys to move.

Attacking: Move the mouse! Left click for shooting egg puffs to the direction of mouse.

Healing: Press 'f' to heal your partner if they are NOT healthy!

Cheat Mode: Press 'shift' + 'C' to activate the cheat mode. Then, you and the BASE gain the secret power to become immortal and time will become 4 times faster so you don't need to defend your BASE for 3 whole minutes.

Dark Mode

Beware of the Dark Mode! In the last 30s, your vision will be significantly reduced by a mask. Use the mouse to move around the mask to view different parts of the map.

Ranking System

After the game is finished, you will see a leaderboard. We rank the players statistics by hit rate, then remaining health.

So, don't spam egg puffs if you want to stand on the leaderboard.
